## What the Business Does

Basketball training facility for **all ages**. Two core audiences:

1. **Young players** — fundamentals (dribbling, passing, teamwork) taught through fun, game-based drills with adjustable hoops.
2. **Older / competitive players** — technique refinement, strategy, performance coaching, and strength programs.

Key differentiator: age-specific training zones, personalized skill assessments and training plans, modern equipment, and a community-driven atmosphere — all under one roof.
